Principles

Built the way software should be

Four rules we do not break, no matter how tempting the analytics dashboard looks.

Privacy is the default, not a setting

Our apps do not collect personal data, do not require an account, and do not embed analytics or advertising SDKs. What happens on your device stays on your device — there is no server to send it to.

Fast, native, tiny

Written in Swift and SwiftUI against Apple's own frameworks. Small downloads, instant launches, full support for dark mode, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.

One job, done properly

No feature creep, no upsell maze. Each app solves a single problem and gets out of your way.

Works without a signal

Everything runs offline. Airplane mode, a dead hotspot or a locked-down network changes nothing.

A human answers

Write to us and a person reads it. Bug reports and feature ideas turn into real updates.

Support

Something broken? Something missing?

Tell us your device model, iOS version and what you expected to happen. We usually reply within two business days.
