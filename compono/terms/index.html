05 Everyday English, including rude words

English as people really use it includes words that some people find rude or offensive. Compono teaches them for that reason:

  • The vocabulary includes some everyday swearing and sexual words. They are taught as words you will hear and read, not as encouragement to use them to insult or harass anyone
  • They are flagged in the App’s content. Flagged words, and phrases that contain one, never appear in a reminder or on the widget, which anyone looking at your iPhone can read. The meanings shown there are not filtered, so a word that is not flagged can still have a meaning that mentions a sensitive subject. Inside the App, flagged words are part of the course like any other word
