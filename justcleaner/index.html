JustCleaner

Speaker water remover for iPhone

Got your iPhone wet? JustCleaner plays a carefully tuned low-frequency tone through the speaker. The vibration pushes trapped droplets out, so your audio stops sounding muffled — usually in under 30 seconds.

How it works

Three steps, thirty seconds

Point the speaker down

Hold your iPhone with the bottom speaker grille facing the floor so gravity helps the water leave.

Tap eject

The app plays a low-frequency tone at high volume. The speaker membrane vibrates and pushes droplets out.

Wipe and repeat

Dry the grille with a soft cloth. If audio still sounds muffled, run the cycle once or twice more.

Features

Small app, no compromises

Tuned frequency sweep

A low-frequency tone in the range that moves the most air through the speaker port, played at a level that stays inside safe output limits for the hardware.

Zero permissions

No microphone, no location, no contacts, no notifications. The app asks for nothing because it needs nothing.

Nothing leaves your phone

No analytics, no crash SDKs, no ad networks. There is no backend to send anything to.

Fast by design

Launches instantly, one screen, one button. No onboarding carousel, no rating nag.

Accessible

Full VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type support, and a layout that respects Reduce Motion.
