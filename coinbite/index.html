How it works

Three taps to somewhere good

CoinBite answers one question: where can I eat well near here, for the money I want to spend? It does not ask who you are, only what you feel like and how much.

Pick a meal

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Coffee & snack. Each one searches for the kind of place that serves it.

Set your budget

How much you want to spend per person, in the currency of the country you are in, or one you choose in Settings. Tap a preset, nudge it with − and +, or type any amount on the keypad.

Get your top five

CoinBite looks for real places around you with Apple Maps, and AI picks the five that fit best, each with an estimated price per person, what to order and why. Tap Directions to go.

Features

Eat well, spend what you meant to

Real places, picked by AI

The places come from Apple Maps, around where you are standing, so every pick is somewhere that exists. AI then weighs them against your meal, your budget, the distance and the time of day, and picks five. Each one comes with an estimated price per person in your currency (and in the local one, when you are abroad), a suggestion of what to order and a line on why it made the list.

No account, nothing kept

No sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your exact location stays on your iPhone: to rank places, the app sends our server a location rounded to about 100 metres, along with the search itself, and the server keeps no copy of it. The privacy policy lists exactly what goes where.

Food preferences

Vegetarian, Vegan, Halal, Gluten-free or Healthy & light, one or several at once. CoinBite looks for places listed for them, and asks the AI to keep every pick and every suggestion within all of them.

Google ratings

Each pick shows its Google Maps rating and how many people rated it, where Google has one.

Directions, your way

Walking directions in Apple Maps, or in Google Maps or Yandex Maps when they are installed. Details opens the place’s Apple Maps card.

35 languages, 33 currencies

The app speaks 35 languages, right-to-left ones included, and the suggestions are written in yours. Budgets work in 33 currencies, from dollars and euros to so‘m, yen and rupees.

Made to feel good

Liquid Glass in emerald and gold, dark by default with light and system looks, a short animation for each meal while it searches, and gentle haptics you can turn off.

Good to know

What it is, and what it is not

Prices are estimates

CoinBite does not read menus. The price per person is the AI’s estimate from the kind of place, the area and local prices, so check the menu before you order.

Not a certification

A food preference is matched on how places are listed and what the AI knows about them. CoinBite never confirms that a place is certified halal or safe for an allergy: ask the place, especially for an allergy.

Opening hours are not checked

CoinBite does not know whether a place is open right now. Check the hours under Details, or in your maps app, before you set off.

AI can be wrong

What to order and why it fits are written by AI, and can be wrong or out of date. A place may have changed its menu, its prices, or closed for good.

Needs location and a connection

CoinBite searches around where you are, so it needs Location access while you use it, and an internet connection. If the AI ranking cannot be reached, it shows the closest places instead, without prices.

iPhone, in portrait

CoinBite 1.0 is made for iPhone, held upright, on iOS 26 or later.
