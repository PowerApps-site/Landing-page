CoinBite helps you choose where to eat. It does not run, inspect or speak for any of the places it shows. Please read the following as part of these Terms rather than as small print:

  • Suggestions are written by AI. What to order, why a place fits and the summary are generated by an AI model and may be wrong, out of date or incomplete. A place may have changed its menu or prices, moved or closed
  • Food preferences are not a certification. Vegetarian, Vegan, Halal, Gluten-free and Healthy & light are matched on how places are listed and what the AI knows about them. If you have an allergy or a dietary or religious requirement, confirm it with the place before you order

  • The App needs Location access and an internet connection, and depends on services run by others (section 5). When one of them is unavailable, the App may show fewer details or none

Important: CoinBite gives suggestions, not guarantees. It is not responsible for the food, the service, the prices or the safety of any place it shows, and nothing in it is medical, dietary or religious advice.

08 Free searches and Premium

CoinBite is free to download. Every iPhone gets 5 free AI searches, once: searches with AI picks, price estimates and Google ratings. After those, searches show the closest places from Apple Maps, without them. The free searches are counted on your iPhone and do not renew.

CoinBite Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription that adds AI picks, price estimates and Google ratings to every search. It comes in three plans:

WeeklyUS$0.99 a week
MonthlyUS$3.99 a month
YearlyUS$39.99 a year, starting with a 7-day free trial for those eligible
  • Premium is sold and processed by Apple through your Apple Account. The App and the App Store show the price in your local currency before you buy, so it can differ from the US dollar prices because of exchange rates and taxes
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase or, with the yearly plan’s free trial, when the trial ends
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends
  • You can manage or cancel it at any time in iOS under Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or from Settings › Premium in the App. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium continues until the end of the period you have paid for
  • The free trial is for the yearly plan only, and Apple decides who is eligible: usually one introductory offer per Apple Account. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription
  • You can restore Premium on your other devices with Restore Purchases in the App, at no extra cost
  • Refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple under its own policies; we cannot issue refunds directly. Request one through reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Premium gives more of the App’s features. It does not change anything in section 4: prices stay estimates and suggestions stay AI-written. While a service in section 5 is unavailable, a search may come back without AI picks or ratings
