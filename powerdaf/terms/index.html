05 Safe use and limits

Live DAF plays your own voice, and masking noise if you turn it on, straight into your headphones. Please use it with care:

  • Use headphones for live DAF. The App will not start it through the iPhone speaker, a car’s audio system, AirPlay or HDMI, because the microphone would pick the sound back up. Wired headphones work best; Bluetooth headphones add a delay of their own
  • Keep the volume moderate. Your own voice should sound a little louder than usual, no more. The App limits its own output and masking levels, but how loud it finally is depends on your iPhone’s volume and on your headphones
  • Take breaks — 10 to 20 minutes at a time is plenty. Ringing or muffled hearing after a session means the volume was too high; stop, and if it does not pass, see a doctor
  • Do not use live DAF or masking noise while driving, cycling, crossing a road, operating machinery or anywhere else you need to hear what is going on around you
  • If a breathing exercise makes you light-headed or short of breath, stop and breathe normally
  • A recording can pick up other people nearby. Record only where that is appropriate and lawful

What the App cannot do

So that there is no confusion about what you are getting, the following are limits of the App, stated as part of these Terms:

  • It does not analyse or judge your speech. It never counts stutters and gives no fluency score; the practice log holds only what you practised, for how long, and what you chose to note
  • The real delay it shows is an estimate — the delay you set plus the latency iOS reports for your microphone and headphones — not a measurement taken at your ear. Bluetooth latency in particular varies from one pair of headphones to another
  • It cannot tell how loud your headphones are, or whether they are on your ears
  • “Find your delay” compares two delays by ear. Its result is your preference on the day, not a clinical setting
  • The reading passages, metronome lines and other practice material are written separately for each language the App supports. They are practice material, not a clinical programme, and they may contain mistakes — if you find one, please tell us
  • Reminders are scheduled by iOS and depend on your notification and Focus settings; the App cannot make sure one arrives

Important: PowerDAF does not promise that any technique, delay or setting will change how you speak, and buying Pro does not change that.
